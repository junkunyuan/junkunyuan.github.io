Drawing

Skill

Sep 21, 2026      Drawing

Create publication-quality architecture diagrams and data charts, guided by the visual clarity of Kaiming He’s papers.

  • Standard. Aim for the visual quality of ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, and Nature: beautiful, refined, and concise. Start from the one message the figure must convey; let every element explain the method or support the comparison.

  • References. Study original figures in Kaiming He’s MAE, MoCo, and MeanFlow. Adapt their composition, abstraction, typography, color roles, and visual hierarchy to the task; use the references to guide design decisions, not as fixed templates.

  • Diagrams. Use simple geometry, aligned modules, balanced whitespace, and unambiguous arrows. Keep the main path easy to follow; use consistent symbols and colors for repeated concepts, reserving emphasis for the core contribution.

  • Charts. Choose the plot that makes the scientific comparison clearest. Preserve the data, label axes and units, and keep scales comparable; use restrained color with distinct markers or line styles so the figure remains readable in grayscale.

  • Finish. Use a clean background and consistent fonts, sizes, and line weights. Inspect at the final publication size for legibility, spacing, and overlaps; deliver editable source and vector PDF or SVG, with a PNG preview.
